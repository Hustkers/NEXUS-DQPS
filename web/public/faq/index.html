D2C is a business model that allows you to sell products without having to buy and stock up inventory beforehand.

Basically, when you get a sale on your store, you provide a supplier with the order details and pay for the product and shipping cost to have the supplier ship the order out for your customers.

This way, you won't need to buy stock in advance and that brings the barrier down for you to test many products and find winners.  

We have a dedicated course about D2C, how to set up a store and start selling that you can watch here.

Ad Library is a database that consists of millions of Facebook ads.

You can view Facebook ads and see their ad spend to determine if the product is profitable or not.

It is the best way to validate if a product is a winner.

Portfolio is a library of hand-picked products that you receive from our team.

On Monday at 12 PM EST every week, a drop containing up to 40 winning products is released to your Portfolio.

We essentially automate the product research for you in Portfolio as we do all the work if you are tight on time.

Competitor Research allows you to see all your competitors in one place, in just a few clicks.

Instead of going to Google to search for your competitors, this tool eliminates that method and saves you a lot of time.
